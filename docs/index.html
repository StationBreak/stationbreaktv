What it is

Turn it on and something is already playing

  • Live

    Live channels from your library

    Your shows and films play on a schedule across themed channels, the way cable does. Tune in part-way through and join whatever is on.

  • Guide

    A guide, and flipping

    Flip channels with the remote and browse what is on now and next. Pause, go back to the start of the show, and skip ahead again.

  • Kids

    Kids channels that stay kids

    Kids channels show only titles made for kids and rated within the kids limit. Unrated titles stay off.

What you need

A server and a Fire TV

  • A Jellyfin server on your network, version 12.0 or later, with your own media on it.
  • A Fire TV device running Fire OS 6 or later. It works with the remote alone.
