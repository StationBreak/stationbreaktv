# Support

Email **[support@stationbreak.tv](mailto:support@stationbreak.tv)**.

To help us help you, include:

- your TV model and its software version (on Fire TV: **Settings → My Fire TV → About**);
- your Jellyfin server's version;
- how the TV reaches the server (plain http at home, https, a reverse proxy at a path such as `/jellyfin`);
- what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead, with any message the app showed.

**Never send your password, sign-in token or server address.** We don't need them to help, and we will never ask for
them.

Many questions are answered in the [guide](guide/index.md) and the [FAQ](guide/faq.md).

## Bugs and ideas

If you have a GitHub account, you can also report a bug or suggest an idea in the
[issue tracker](https://github.com/StationBreak/stationbreaktv/issues). Issues there are public: leave out anything
about your server or household.

## Security problems

Don't email security problems or post them in a public issue. Report them privately as the
[security policy](https://github.com/StationBreak/stationbreaktv/blob/main/SECURITY.md) describes: on the repository's
**Security** tab, choose **Report a vulnerability**.
