# Channels

## Built-in channels

After you choose libraries, the app builds a lineup of built-in channels from what is in them: themed channels
based on genre, rating and similar metadata. Each show or film gets one home channel, so the same title doesn't turn
up everywhere. A channel with nothing to air today is hidden from the guide and comes back when it has something.

The schedule is worked out on each TV from your library, and a new day's schedule starts at 6 AM.

## The Channels screen

Open it from the guide's menu: **Channels**. Channels are grouped as **On the air**, **More channels**
(channels that didn't make the lineup) and **Switched off**. Select a channel to:

- **Watch** it;
- **Copy to customise** a built-in channel. Built-in channels can't be edited: a copy becomes your own channel that you
  edit freely. A copy of **After Hours** is always an adult channel and can't be changed to anything else; copying it is
  only for Pro;
- **Switch on** or **Switch off** your own channels. Keep at least one channel on;
- **Change number** of your own channels. If another of your own channels has the number, the app offers to **Swap**
  the two. **Default number** puts it back.

Channel numbers and switched-off channels are kept per TV.

### Custom channels

You can make up to 5 channels of your own (a copy of a built-in channel counts as one). To make another, delete one
first. The built-in lineup is chosen for your library. Switching built-in channels on or off, renumbering them,
resetting your channels, making a sixth channel and copying After Hours are part of Pro. Choose one on the free app
and a note says it is part of Pro. Where the store is connected the note has **See Pro**, which opens the Pro screen
(Back returns you to where you were) and **OK**; where it isn't, the note only has **OK**. Nothing is refused without
saying why. Your own channels can be switched on and off and renumbered freely, and can swap
numbers with each other. A number a built-in channel holds is taken.

**Use my collections** can be switched on or off at any time.

## Build your own channel

Choose **New channel**. Three steps:

1. **Sources**: what can air. Libraries, Collections, Tags, Genres, or **Choose shows and films** by name. Then
   **Only include** (genres, years, runtime, only films or only shows, a rating limit) and **Leave out** anything you
   don't want.
2. **How it plays**:
   - **Shuffle**: random order; no show plays more episodes in a row than its limit while others wait.
   - **Rotation**: shows and collections take turns, each keeping its place.
   - **In order**: one show start to finish, then the next, oldest first.

   You can cap **Episodes in a row** and **Films of a collection in a row**.
3. **Name and number**.

A preview shows what would air. **Save and watch** tunes to the new channel. To change it later, select it in
**Channels** and choose **Edit**, or delete it there.

## Tots channels

Tots channels (numbers 1 to 33) show only titles made for kids and rated within the Tots limit. Unrated titles are left out. This holds
whatever the channel's sources say: the builder lists what it skipped ("Not made for kids", "Rated above the Tots
limit").

Turn on **Tots channel** in the builder to make your own. Tots channels use their own band of numbers. The words for
the bands and for Family Mode are in the [glossary](glossary.md).
