# Free and Pro

StationBreak is a free app you can use every day without paying. **Pro** is a one-time purchase that adds the ways to
change your lineup, and a few conveniences for parents. Pro comes after the free app is out, and it is not on sale yet.

## What's free

- The channels StationBreak builds from your library, up to 50, refreshed every morning at 6 AM. You can open and browse
  every channel, including built-in channels that aren't in your lineup right now.
- Up to **5 channels of your own**. Deleting one is always free.
- **Use my collections**, choosing your libraries, and any kind of Jellyfin server.
- The kids rules in [Mature content and kids](mature-content.md): kids channels hold back anything that isn't made for
  kids, and **After Hours** never turns up by itself. Kids mode with a PIN, the Kids, Family and Teen levels, and the
  fall-back to the restricted mode after the TV sits idle will be free too. Keeping children safe isn't a paid feature.

## What Pro adds

- Switch on built-in channels that aren't in your lineup, switch built-in channels off, hide them and renumber them.
- **Reset** your lineup back to the one StationBreak builds.
- More than 5 channels of your own, and copying **After Hours**.
- Later, for parents: hours of operation (a weekly pattern of which levels apply when), a bedtime card with optional
  white noise, and a holiday or sick-day mode.

Pro is bought once and doesn't expire. Nothing in the free app is taken away when Pro arrives.

## If a purchase is refunded

Pro stops, and your lineup is tidied the way it would be on the free app:

- Built-in channels go back to the lineup StationBreak builds. Your changes to them (switch-ons, switch-offs, hidden
  channels and numbers) are not kept. If you buy Pro again you set them up again.
- Your 5 oldest channels of your own keep working. Any others are **locked, not deleted**: they stay in your lineup
  file and come back if you buy Pro again.

## Where Pro is sold

Paid Pro is sold on Amazon, for Fire TV, and nowhere else. On the Amazon version, once its store is connected, **Settings → StationBreak Pro** shows
what Pro adds and the price the store gives, with **Buy Pro** and **Restore purchases** (after a reinstall, or on a
second TV with the same Amazon account). Until then the app shows nothing about Pro. It is also offered, once, when you choose something that is part of Pro, such
as switching a built-in channel off. Nothing pops up by itself, and it is never shown in Kids Mode.

The GitHub download of StationBreak is open source, and it has Pro unlocked. Its **Settings → Support StationBreak**
page shows where to help the project, and it never nags.
