# Glossary

## Family Mode, Tots, Kids and Teens

**Family Mode** is the setting, from 1 to 99, that keeps the TV to the family-friendly channels. It is the setting that
used to be called Kids Mode. It has a PIN, and its limit is one of three **bands** of channel numbers:

| Name | Channel numbers | What it holds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Tots** | 1 to 33 | Channels made for young children. The default limit of Family Mode. |
| **Kids** | 34 to 66 | Family channels. A Family Mode limit of Kids shows Tots and Kids. |
| **Teens** | 67 to 99 | Channels for teens. A limit of Teens shows Tots, Kids and Teens. |

The limits add up: Kids includes Tots, and Teens includes both. Every other channel (100 and up) is for grown-ups and is
hidden while Family Mode is on. See [Mature content and kids](mature-content.md).

**Old names.** Before November 2026 the setting was **Kids Mode**, the 1 to 33 band was **Kids**, and the 34 to 66 band
was **Family**. Where an older document, issue or decision record says "Kids" for 1 to 33 or "Family" for 34 to 66, it
means Tots and Kids today. Titles "made for kids" is a different use: it describes a title, not a band.

**Names that did not change.** Inside the app and the project's decision records some names keep the old words, because
they are stored on the TV or frozen in test files: `KidsLock`, `KidsCap` (its values `Kids`, `Family` and `Teen`),
`Band.Kids` and `Band.Family`, the `kidsOnly` pool policy, `kid-hold-*`, and the `kids-odds-and-ends` channel (the
Tots' Odds & Ends channel, 33).

| Code name | Product name |
| --- | --- |
| Kids Mode (`KidsLock`) | Family Mode |
| `Band.Kids` (1 to 33) | Tots |
| `Band.Family` (34 to 66) | Kids |
| `Band.Teen` (67 to 99) | Teens |
